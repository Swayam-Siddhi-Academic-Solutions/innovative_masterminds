WHAT IT LOOKS AT

A quick snapshot of the student’s learning needs.

This is not an exam. It is designed to identify areas where guidance, practice or counselling may be useful.

How comfortable the learner feels with concepts, tests and independent problem-solving.

∑Aptitude Readiness

Online or offline, one-to-one or small group, preferred learning style and support needs.
